Privacy Policy

Hole in the Wall: Pose Run · Android · Last updated: 22 September 2026

This policy explains what Hole in the Wall: Pose Run (the “Game”, package com.yassin.holeinthewall) does and does not do with your information. It covers the Android version distributed through Google Play.

The Game is made and published by Yassin Mohamed Mahmoud (“we”, “us”). You can reach us at historava.app@gmail.com.

The short version

The Game shows advertisements and measures how it is played. There is no account and no login, and we never ask you for your name, your email address or anything else about you. Your progress is saved on your own device. Two Google services — Google AdMob, which supplies the advertisements, and Google Analytics for Firebase, which counts how the Game is played — do collect information from your device, and this page explains what.

Information we collect ourselves

None. We operate no servers and have no database. The Game never asks for, and we never receive, your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files or location.

Information collected by Google on our behalf

The Game includes two Google software kits. We see only their pooled, anonymous reports — never a list of people, and never anything that names you.

Google AdMob serves the advertisements. To choose an advertisement and to stop the same one repeating, it may collect your device’s advertising ID, your approximate location derived from your IP address, your device model and operating system version, and whether you watched or tapped an advertisement. Google’s own explanation of this is at support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543, and how Google uses data from apps that use its services is at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Google Analytics for Firebase counts how the Game is played, so we can see which parts of it work. It records events such as a run starting, a run ending with its score and how many walls were passed, a new world opening, and whether an advertisement was shown and its reward taken. It also collects an app-instance identifier, your device model, operating system version, and your country. Firebase’s description of this data is at firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

We do not sell your information, and we do not combine it with anything else to work out who you are.

Advertising choices

You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your phone’s Settings → Privacy → Ads, which stops advertisements being personalised to it.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the Game asks for your consent before any advertisement is requested, using Google’s own consent form. Your answer is remembered and you can change it later from within the Game.

Information stored on your device

To remember your progress, the Game keeps one small save file inside its own private storage on your device. It holds only game data:

  • your best score and total points, and which worlds you have opened;
  • your coins and gems, the items you own and the items you are wearing;
  • your daily gift progress;
  • your sound and music settings.

This file is never sent anywhere. Other apps cannot read it. Uninstalling the Game, or clearing its storage in your phone’s settings, deletes it permanently.

In-game purchases

Everything in the Game’s shop is bought with coins and gems earned by playing. There are no purchases with real money.

Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13 and is listed on Google Play for ages 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided such information, write to us at the address below and we will act on it.

Your rights

Because we hold no information about you ourselves, there is nothing for us to show you or delete. To deal with the data the Google services above collect, use the advertising controls described earlier, Google’s own privacy tools at myaccount.google.com/privacy, or uninstall the Game, which stops any further collection.

Google Play, which you use to download the Game, has its own privacy policy, which applies to your use of the store: policies.google.com/privacy.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the Game changes any of the above, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.
