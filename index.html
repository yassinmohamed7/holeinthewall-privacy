Privacy Policy

Hole in the Wall: Pose Run · Android · Last updated: 17 September 2026

This policy explains what Hole in the Wall: Pose Run (the “Game”, package com.yassin.holeinthewall) does and does not do with your information. It covers the Android version distributed through Google Play.

The Game is made and published by Yassin Mohamed Mahmoud (“we”, “us”). You can reach us at historava.app@gmail.com.

The short version

The Game collects nothing. There is no account, no login, no advertising and no analytics, and the Game does not connect to the internet at all. Your progress is saved on your own device and never leaves it.

Information we collect

None. We operate no servers and have no database. The Game never asks for, and we never receive, your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files, location, device identifiers or any other personal information.

Information stored on your device

To remember your progress, the Game keeps one small save file inside its own private storage on your device. It holds only game data:

  • your best score and total points, and which worlds you have opened;
  • your coins and gems, the items you own and the items you are wearing;
  • your daily gift progress;
  • your sound and music settings.

This file is never sent anywhere. Other apps cannot read it. Uninstalling the Game, or clearing its storage in your phone’s settings, deletes it permanently.

Internet, advertising and third parties

The current version of the Game does not request the internet permission, shows no advertisements and contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking services. We do not share or sell any information, because we have none to share.

Google Play, which you use to download the Game, has its own privacy policy, which applies to your use of the store: policies.google.com/privacy.

In-game purchases

Everything in the Game’s shop is bought with coins and gems earned by playing. There are no purchases with real money.

Children

The Game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13, because it does not collect personal information at all.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the Game changes any of the above — for example by adding advertisements — this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.

Contact
